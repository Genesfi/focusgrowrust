[1] 🌐 FOCUSGROW BROWSER EXTENSION (RESTRICTED SITES GUARD):
------------------------------------------------------------------------
To block distracting websites (YouTube, Reddit, Instagram, Twitter, etc.)
directly inside your browser during Focus sessions and use Emergency Passes:

1. Open your browser's Extension Manager:
   - Google Chrome : chrome://extensions
   - Microsoft Edge: edge://extensions
   - Brave Browser : brave://extensions

2. Toggle ON "Developer mode" in the top-right corner.

3. Click "Load unpacked" and select the "extension-focusgrow" folder inside
   the FocusGrow installation directory (or use the shortcut in Start Menu).

4. FocusGrow will automatically sync your restricted websites in real time!

[2] 🎵 MUSIC & MEDIA SYNC (SPOTIFY & YOUTUBE MUSIC):
------------------------------------------------------------------------
FocusGrow supports real-time media sync with spinning vinyl disc, live album
accent themes, and playback controls:

A. Spotify & Desktop Players (Native - Zero Setup):
   - Just open Spotify Desktop or any Windows media player.
   - FocusGrow automatically detects song titles, artist, and playback status!

B. YouTube Music & Browser Extensions (Embedded Server :8765):
   - FocusGrow natively includes its own embedded WebSocket server on :8765.
   - No separate server process (YTMPX) is needed!
   - Simply load the "extension-ytmpx" folder into your browser via
     "Load unpacked" in chrome://extensions.
   - You can also toggle Media Sync on/off anytime in FocusGrow Settings.
